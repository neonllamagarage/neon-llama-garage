THE NEON LLAMA STORY

Neon Llama Garage is a family-owned, father-and-son team operating out of dual workshops. Avid collectors since 1995, we got tired of staring at the same traditional race cars on the pegs.

Tim handles the vehicle sourcing, drilling, stripping, and custom painting in the Utah shop, while the Texas base manages the precision decals, packaging, and order fulfillment. We build exactly what we want to see on our own walls: visually disruptive, high-impact customs designed to stand out in a display of thousands.
